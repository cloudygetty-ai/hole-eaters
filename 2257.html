Everyone shown must be 18+ and consenting

Hole Eaters is for adults 18 and over only. Under our Terms, members may post only content in which every person shown was at least 18 when it was made and consented to it being made and shared. Report content

If you believe anything on Hole Eaters shows a minor, or anyone who didn't consent, report it in the app or email cloudygetty@gmail.com. We review every report and remove violating content.
