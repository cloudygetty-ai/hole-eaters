Safety on Hole Eaters

Last updated November 26, 2026

Hole Eaters is a live map for adults to meet people nearby. This page explains, in plain words, how we protect your location, how to block or report someone, and how to meet safely. It's for members, first-timers, and the bars and partners who put our name on their walls.

  • Nobody sees your exact location. Your pin sits in a grid square about 200 m (650 ft) across, and distances are rounded to the nearest 100 m.
  • Blocking works both ways. You disappear from each other's map, and they can't message, like or review you.
  • Reports are private. Only you and our moderators can see a report. The person you report is never told.
  • 18+ only. Anyone found to be under 18 is removed, and child abuse material is reported to the authorities.

How your location is blurred

The map never shows where you actually are. Before anyone else sees you, your position is snapped to a grid square about 200 m (650 ft) across, and your pin goes in the middle of that square. Distances are measured from that pin, rounded to the nearest 100 m, and never shown as less than 100 m.

  • Distance off: in Settings → Privacy, turn on Distance to hide your pin and distance completely. You still show in Nearby, without either.
  • Ghost Mode: takes you off the map and out of Nearby altogether. Use it at home, at work, or anywhere you'd rather not be found.
  • No email, no pin: people browsing without an account don't appear on the map at all.
  • Uploads are cleaned: location data is stripped from photos and videos on your phone, before they upload.
  • Chats are private: messages are end-to-end encrypted, and photos sent in a chat open only for the two of you, through links that expire.

Want more privacy? Leave your street, building and workplace out of your profile, and turn on Blur photos to blur photos you receive until you tap them.

Blocking someone

Open their profile, go to the Report tab and tap Block User. The block is saved on our servers, not just your phone, and works both ways:

  • They disappear from your map and Nearby, and you disappear from theirs.
  • They can't message you, send you photos, like you, check out your profile, start a new chat or leave you a review.

We don't tell them they've been blocked. To undo a block, contact us.

Reporting someone

Open their profile, go to the Report tab, pick a reason, add any details and tap Submit Report. If you also want to block them, report first: blocking closes their profile.

Only you and our moderators can see your report. We review each one and may remove content, suspend or ban accounts. Anyone we find to be under 18 is removed, and we report suspected child sexual abuse material to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC), as the law requires.

Can't report in the app, or it's about something off the app? Contact us. If you're in danger, call 911 first. We can't send help.

Meeting someone

  • Chat first. Keep it in the app until you're comfortable. A quick video call is the easiest way to check someone is who they say.
  • Meet in public the first time: a bar, a coffee shop, somewhere with people around.
  • Tell a friend who you're meeting, where and when. Share your live location from your phone's own settings.
  • Get yourself there and home. Keep your phone charged and some cash for a ride.
  • Keep your drink in sight. Go easy on alcohol and drugs, and don't mix them.
  • Consent can change at any time. Anyone can say no, or stop, at any point.
  • Play safer. Bring condoms, and ask a clinic about PrEP. If a condom broke or you're worried, ask about PEP right away: it has to start within 72 hours.
  • Trust your gut. If something feels off, leave. You don't owe anyone an explanation.

Help in Philadelphia and nationwide

  • Emergency: call 911.
  • Sexual assault: RAINN's National Sexual Assault Hotline, free and confidential 24/7: call 800-656-4673 or text HOPE to 64673.
  • Mental health crisis: call or text 988.
  • Testing, PrEP and PEP: Mazzoni Center offers HIV and STI testing, PrEP and PEP at 1348 Bainbridge St and at its Washington West clinic, 1201 Locust St. Check hours before you go.

Adults only

You must be 18 or older to use Hole Eaters. If someone looks younger, report them with Underage. Our 2257 statement explains our rules for photos.

For bars and partners

If you put Hole Eaters on your walls, here's what you can tell your customers:

  • It's free and adults-only. Nobody's exact location is ever shown, including inside your venue.
  • People can browse without an email, and they don't appear on the map until they make an account.
  • Members can block or report anyone from their profile, and we review every report.

If a problem at your venue involves Hole Eaters, or you want posters, stickers or QR cards, contact us.
