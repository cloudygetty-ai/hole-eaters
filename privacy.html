How we use it

Only to run Hole Eaters: showing profiles and the map, delivering messages and notifications, preventing abuse, enforcing our Terms, and fixing problems. We don't use your data for advertising and we don't build marketing profiles.

Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent personal data. We use these providers to run the service, and they process data only on our behalf:

  • Supabase: database, file storage and sign-in
  • Vercel: website hosting
  • Fly.io: real-time and background services
  • Resend: email alerts, only if you turn them on
  • Apple, Google, Mozilla push services: delivering notifications to your browser
  • OpenFreeMap (map tiles) and Google Fonts: these see your IP address when your browser loads map images or fonts
  • Google Maps: only if you open Street View

We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect someone from serious harm. Because chats are end-to-end encrypted, we can't hand over their contents.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account exists.

When you delete your account, everything is deleted immediately and permanently: your profile, photos, videos, chats (including chat files on both sides), likes, views, reviews, blocks, check-ins, public room posts, location, settings, push subscriptions and sign-in record. There is no grace period and we keep no copy, unless preservation is required by law: for example a subpoena, warrant or court order, a legal hold for litigation, or apparent child sexual abuse material, which federal law (18 U.S.C. § 2258A) requires us to preserve and report to NCMEC.

Two things stay, with your identity removed: reports you filed about other members (so we can keep acting on them), and venues you added to the map. Deleted data may persist for a short time in our hosting provider's routine encrypted backups until they're overwritten.

Your choices and rights

  • Ghost Mode: hide yourself from the map and Nearby
  • Hide Distance and Hide Age: keep them off your profile
  • Stealth: view profiles without them knowing
  • Location: turn it off in your browser or phone settings at any time
  • Edit or remove any profile detail or photo whenever you like
  • Delete your account: Settings → Privacy → Delete my account

Depending on where you live (for example California, other US states, the UK or the EU), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your data, and to object to certain processing. Email cloudygetty@gmail.com and we'll respond within 30 days. We won't treat you differently for using these rights.

Security

Chats are end-to-end encrypted, all traffic uses HTTPS, and database access rules stop members from reading anything they shouldn't. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we'll tell you as the law requires.
